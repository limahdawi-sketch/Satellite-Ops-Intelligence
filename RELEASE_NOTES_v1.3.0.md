# Satellite Ops Intelligence v1.3.0

## Added
- Direction-aware downlink/uplink illustrative presets with automatic recalculation and an explicit profile reload button.
- Link-budget formula guide that explains every principal equation and symbol.
- Combined C/N or C/I result explanation with the reciprocal-linear combining formula and limitations.
- Synchronized HPA saturation output fields in dBW and watts, supported by tested conversion functions.
- Complete plain-language formula guides for spectrum occupancy, bandwidth budget, HPA power budget, loading, net rate, annual availability arithmetic, outage reduction and remaining fade margin.
- Clearer Phase 02 AI Troubleshooting and Phase 03 Operator Intelligence deliverables/validation gates.
- Larger text for in-page field helpers, results, tables, formulas and roadmap cards.

## Engineering limits retained
- Uplink/downlink values are illustrative examples, not operator-specific measurements.
- Combined impairment results require consistent reference carrier, bandwidth and measurement point.
- Capacity is a screening model; it does not simulate payload nonlinearity/intermodulation or certify sellable capacity.
- Rain-fade chart is a linear what-if model, not an ITU-R P.618 prediction.
