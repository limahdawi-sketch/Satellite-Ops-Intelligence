## English

**From Satellite RF Equations to Engineering Decisions 🚀**

I’m sharing a self-initiated project: **Satellite Ops Intelligence** — a transparent, interactive learning and engineering-screening lab.

Its new Guided Learning walkthrough follows a synthetic 30 Mbps downlink example through FSPL, C/N₀, Eb/N₀ and residual margin, then separately screens an illustrative transponder plan and visualizes how a fade-depth assumption changes the remaining margin.

The example is intentionally transparent: an 8.70 dB residual link margin under the stated assumptions; 28.5 MHz occupied from 34 MHz available; and a simplified HPA output budget. These are teaching values, not live operator data or certified engineering results.

The goal is to make equations, units, assumptions and model boundaries visible—so engineers can compare scenarios and prepare clearer decision briefs.
